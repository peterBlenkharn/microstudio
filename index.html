What it is

An intensive, collaborative creative project for our undergraduates — now in its third year, building and launching original student games on Steam in just 12 weeks.

What it's for

Building real-world skills, getting published on Steam, and pushing creative boundaries — one unified project, three specialist subteams, professional support.

Who it's for

BSc Games Development students working alongside lecturers, industry creatives, and each other to ship a game on Steam.

Dream Box

Working Title

First-Person Surrealist Exploration

"The room remembers what you forgot."

A first-person surrealist exploration game set entirely within a single room — the inside of someone's mind, infected by digital noise. The room transforms around you using dream logic as you interact with corrupted domestic objects.

Surreal over Real

Symbolic logic, collage-based aesthetics, Digital Dadaism.

Aggressive Scope Control

One room, no external scenes — the journey is the transformation.

Diegetic Immersion

Minimal UI — the world tells you what you need to know.

Strangeness and Simplicity

Simple but strange — provocative mechanics over complex systems.

Unity 6 (URP) Windows PC Steam 60–90 min

Frequently asked questions

Micro Studio is an extracurricular programme at the University of Greenwich where selected BSc Games Development students form teams and build a complete game in 12 weeks, with the goal of launching on Steam. Students work alongside lecturers, professional creatives, and each other in a real studio environment.
Students on the BSc Games Development programme can apply or be invited based on their skills, enthusiasm, and commitment. The programme is competitive but inclusive — we look for passion and willingness to learn as much as technical ability.
Absolutely. Our graduates leave with real shipping experience, teamwork skills, and a published game on their CV. Get in touch with us to discuss hiring opportunities, internships, or placement partnerships.
We welcome support in many forms: mentoring, guest talks, tool or software sponsorship, funding, and industry connections. Visit our Partners section or contact us directly to discuss how you can help.
Micro Studio is extracurricular — it runs alongside the regular degree programme. Students participate voluntarily for the experience, portfolio building, and the opportunity to ship a real game. It is not formally assessed, though the skills gained directly complement their coursework.
